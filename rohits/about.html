About us

Delivering excellence, not just goods.

At Rohit's Supply And Logistics, we don't just deliver goods; we deliver excellence. We are a growth-oriented company with a relentless commitment to providing top-notch distribution services. Our founder and proprietor, Rohit Rathi, leads our dedicated team of Store Managers, Transporters, and Loaders, who collectively possess over 12 years of experience in the logistics industry. Our unwavering dedication to efficiency, even under high-pressure, target-driven schedules, ensures that we consistently achieve maximum fill rates within stipulated timeframes.

Our mission

To empower businesses with unparalleled distribution solutions: precision, reliability and innovation in every shipment, tailored to each client's needs so products reach their destination on time, every time.

Our vision

To be the beacon of transformation in the logistics industry, defined by efficiency, sustainability, and client-centricity, setting new standards of excellence across the sector.
